Pending: stronger database durability, multi-user access, synchronization, audit/compliance and broader colony-operation acceptance. Do not put colony records in public demos.

Do not claim novel statistics or ownership of the third-party model. The optional pack now ships a local neural translation model with attribution; explain its limits honestly.
